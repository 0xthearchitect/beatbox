# Security

BeatBox is made for breaking things, so most of what happens inside your
machines is the point, not a bug. What we do want to hear about is BeatBox
itself failing to keep a line it draws.

## Report it privately

Use **Report a vulnerability** on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/0xthearchitect/beatbox/security) of this
repository. Please don't open a public issue for it.

Tell us the BeatBox version (Settings → About), your system, and the steps
to make it happen. We'll get back to you, and we'll let you know when a fix
is out.

## Worth reporting

- A machine reaching your computer, or another machine, in a way BeatBox
  says it can't.
- A firewall rule or a network setting that doesn't do what it shows.
- Another user on the same computer being able to drive your BeatBox.
- A service from the Library, or one you described, running anything on
  your computer instead of inside its machine.
- BeatBox writing to a disk you gave it.

## Versions

Only the latest release gets security fixes.
