# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help.

We don't take pull requests yet. What helps most right now is an issue:
something that broke, something that confused you, or something you wish
BeatBox did. Pick the form that fits and fill it in.

If you'd like to dig into the code, [the README](README.md#build-it-yourself)
says how to build it. `scripts/local_ci.sh` runs every check the project
runs, on your own computer.
